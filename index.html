    Terms — sneaker glossary
    Kicks
    Sneakers. Just another word for shoes — and what this site is named after.
    Heat
    A very desirable, hyped pair that lots of people want.
    Grail
    Your most sought-after pair — usually rare or expensive, the one you'd love to own above all others.
    Deadstock / DS
    Brand new, never worn, usually with the original box.
    VNDS
    Very near deadstock — tried on or worn very briefly, still looks almost new.
    PADS
    Pass as deadstock — worn a little but clean enough to look new.
    OG / OG all
    Original release — or, as “OG all”, a pair that comes with everything it originally came with: original box, extra laces, tags.
    GR / General Release
    A widely available release that most stores carried in good numbers.
    QS / Quickstrike
    A more limited release, dropped with less stock and less warning than a GR.
    Cop / Drop
    Cop means buy a pair; drop means pass on it — don't buy.
    Flip
    Resell a pair quickly for profit, usually soon after buying it.
    Bricks
    Hyped pairs bought to resell that end up not selling, or not making any money.
    W / L
    Win / loss on a release — a W means you got the pair, an L means you missed out.
    Restock
    When a sold-out pair comes back in stock and goes on sale again.
    LC / Legit check
    Checking whether a pair is authentic — real, not fake.
    TTS / True to size
    The shoe fits as you'd expect for its labelled size — not running big or small.

      Who pays shipping? (marketplace rows)

        See why — fee details & rules

        Shipping cost you pay is for marketplace rows — StockX and GOAT use their own shipping / label fees instead. Shipping charged to buyer is set to $0 automatically with free shipping. StockX Level 1 = 9% transaction + 3% processing; higher levels need more quarterly volume. Every GOAT seller starts at 90; seller approval is required to sell on GOAT. Athletic shoes (eBay): 8% with no per-order fee on athletic shoes sold for $150 or more, instead of 13.6% + $0.40. Depop Boost adds 12% of the item price, off by default.

        Net kept = sale price + shipping charged − platform fees − your shipping cost. Profit = net kept − your item cost.

        Marketplace fees are charged on item price + shipping charged, except Poshmark (item price only) and Vinted (no seller fee). With free shipping, the fee base is the item price alone and your shipping cost still comes out of your profit.

        StockX and GOAT rows work differently and ignore the generic shipping inputs: StockX Verified deducts its percentage fee plus its own $5 seller shipping fee; StockX Listings deducts $0; GOAT deducts commission, its label fee ($5 prepaid / $0 drop-off) and the 2.9% cash-out.

        StockX Listings access: 0% seller fees at launch (StockX's June 24, 2026 launch announcement — sellers keep 100% of the sale price), still listed at 0% on Nov 17, 2026. Access is limited to a curated group of US sellers on iOS only, identity verification required, everyone else joins a waitlist. The 0% fee may change — re-check at publish time before counting on it.
        Assumption flagged: Whatnot's fee base wasn't stated in the fee research, so this calculator applies Whatnot's 8% commission + 2.9% + $0.30 processing to item price + shipping charged, like most other platforms. Treat Whatnot numbers as an estimate until that's confirmed.
